Changing or cancelling a flight

Open More → My Bookings, choose the flight and tap Cancel booking. The app shows exactly what the airline will refund before you confirm. For changes, contact the airline with your booking reference (PNR).

Booked on an airline or hotel website?

Those bookings are managed by the airline or hotel. {{ app_name }} can save the confirmation to your itinerary (More → Add a Booking), but changes and refunds go through the company you booked with.

Delete my data

In the app: Settings → Delete my data. This removes your traveler record from our servers immediately.
